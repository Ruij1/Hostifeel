# Hostifeel – Home Services & Real Estate Platform

## Overview
Hostifeel is a public digital platform designed to centralize services, businesses, and tools related to the home industry, including real estate, home services, and home-related stores.

## Problem
Many small and medium businesses lack:
- Online visibility
- A centralized platform to showcase their services
- Simple tools to attract and manage clients

Existing solutions are often fragmented, expensive, or not tailored for non-technical users.

## Solution
Hostifeel provides an all-in-one platform where businesses can:
- Publish properties for sale or rent
- Promote home services and professional advisors
- Showcase home-related stores
- Use tools and calculators designed for the home industry
